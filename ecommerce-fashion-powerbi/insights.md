# Business Insights Summary

## Sales Performance
- Total revenue reached **324K** with a **43.5% profit margin**, indicating strong pricing and cost control.
- Revenue and profit peaked in **May**, followed by a noticeable decline in June.
- Germany and France are the top revenue-generating countries.

---

## Profitability & Discounts
- Discounted sales account for **~7%** of total revenue.
- Discounts contribute to volume but have a limited impact on overall profitability.
- Website banners and app campaigns drive the majority of profit.

---

## Product Performance
- Revenue is concentrated among a small group of top-performing products.
- Bottom 5 products generate minimal revenue and may require:
  - Promotion
  - Bundling
  - Potential discontinuation

---

## Customer Insights
- Total customers: **580**
- Average revenue per customer: **€559**
- Customers are evenly distributed across age ranges, with no single dominant group.
- Germany has the largest customer base.

---

## Customer Value Segmentation
- Majority of customers fall into the **Low-Value segment**
- A smaller group of **High-Value customers** contributes disproportionately to revenue

---

## Strategic Recommendations
- Focus retention efforts on high-value customers
- Optimize discount strategy to protect margins
- Investigate June performance decline
- Reevaluate low-performing products
